Notification channels

E-mail

On when recipients and an SMTP host are set; clear both to switch it off.

Webhook

Receives alerts and a stream of security events as JSON. Clear the URL to switch it off.

Heartbeat

Pinged every five minutes while monitoring works. A dead man's switch service (healthchecks.io style, period 5 minutes) tells you when the pings stop, the one failure the server cannot report itself.

What gets sent

Internet background noise (WAF blocks, bans, ssh brute force) never leaves the interface. Alerts go out once a minute as one message, the same alert at most once an hour unless it turns critical.
